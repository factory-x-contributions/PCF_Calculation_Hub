Carbon intensity (gCO₂/kWh)

Used for AAS-based PCF calculation. Choose a constant value or fetch from Green Grid Compass.

Applied when using constant carbon intensity.

Product Carbon Footprint (PCF) report

When enabled, material carbon footprints from SiGREEN (production + distribution stages) are added to the total PCF reported to SiGREEN. When disabled, only process energy (BOP) is included.
